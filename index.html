AI Visibility Checker

Can AI find, read and cite your page?

Paste a URL. The checker reads the page the way crawlers do, without running JavaScript, and checks the signals that decide whether search engines and AI assistants can use it.

What it checks

Can AI find it?

Whether the page may be indexed, which search and AI crawlers robots.txt lets in, HTTPS and redirects, the sitemap, and llms.txt.

Can AI read it?

Whether the words are in the HTML before any script runs, the title and headings, image alt text, the mobile viewport, and mixed content.

Can AI understand and cite it?

Structured data (JSON-LD), the canonical URL, the description, and the link preview.

How the score works

Each check is worth points, weighted by how much it affects whether a page can be found and cited: being allowed in search and readable without JavaScript count the most, llms.txt the least. A pass earns all of a check's points, "Improve" earns half, and "Fix" earns none. "Fix first" lists the three changes worth the most points. If a site refuses unknown bots, the checker says so and checks only its public files rather than guessing.

A score is a checklist, not a promise: no tool can guarantee a ranking or a citation. The checker fetches public pages only and keeps nothing.
